Made for this project
---------------------
Terrain, rivers, waterfalls, all buildings, the bridge, pagoda, torii, watchtower,
shrine, boat, lanterns, the shoji texture and the moon texture were made in Blender
for this project (blender/scripts/). The stone river walls, street props, ground cover,
kawara roof tiling, rock strata and the ninja's animation are generated in code
(src/world/, src/game/proc/). All sound and music is synthesised in code at startup
(src/audio/): no recordings or third-party audio are used.
